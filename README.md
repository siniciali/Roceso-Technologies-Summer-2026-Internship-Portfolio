# Roceso Technologies — Summer 2026 Internship Portfolio

Index of deliverables produced during a 6-week internship at Roceso Technologies (Singapore, July–August 2026), under the Laidlaw Research and Leadership Programme. Each linked repository is private, with its own README documenting scope, status, and validation.

All deliverables below were reviewed and validated by Jane Wang, CEO of Roceso Technologies.

## Deliverables

| Repository | Area | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| `Roceso_Technologies_Game_Design` | Product / Rehab Design | Design and rationale for motor-cognitive coupled rehabilitation games on EsoGLOVE and CygniSENSE Motion. Work on an agency-maximising, memory-driven architecture. |

## Context

The internship was supervised by Jane Wang and framed around the Laidlaw Programme's dual mandate of research and leadership in action. Work spanned seven areas:

- **Regulatory & quality** — Onboarded into Roceso's Quality Management System (QMS) documentation aligned with ISO 13485: quality procedures, work instructions, inspection criteria, and traceability requirements.
- **Hardware assembly** — Hands-on, end-to-end assembly of EsoGLOVE control box units against formal Work Instructions: mechanical build, wiring and crimping, PCBA mounting, firmware flashing, and functional test, run to the same quality checkpoints used in production.
- **Rehabilitation game design** — Designed a 4-game concept portfolio for CygniSENSE Motion, connecting sense-of-agency and memory-recall mechanisms (grounded in prior published neuroscience research) to a two-layer game architecture, benchmarked against competitor platforms (Gloreha, Neofect, Amadeo).
- **Individuated-finger EMG R&D** — Scoped design constraints and system requirements for individuated finger EMG sensing on EsoGLOVE, including a handoff writeup for the incoming EMG engineer.
- **Market & competitive research** — Produced a structured competitor analysis of the individuated-finger rehabilitation glove market for use in distributor discussions.
- **Clinical & community engagement** — Beyond desk-based design work, spent time directly with patients and clinical partners:
  - Assisted with in-office stroke patient visits for EsoGLOVE use.
  - Visited S3 (Stroke Support Station) to present the research case for memory-based rehabilitation games and their potential to improve motor relearning efficiency, not only cognitive outcomes; also spent time in their stroke rehab gym and a patient kickboxing class, getting direct feedback from stroke patients on their needs.
  - Volunteered with Club HEAL's recovery wellness program, running a computer-use class for participants managing conditions such as dementia, schizophrenia, and depression as part of their mental recovery journey.
- **Internal tooling & operations** — Beyond the five listed repositories, supported Roceso's operations in three further capacities:
  - For a three-week period, ran the operational side of Viely's compression stockings business end-to-end: patient contact and scheduling, order shipping/packaging and receiving, taking leg measurements for patients with circulatory conditions, clinic delivery visits, and direct coordination with clinicians and patients.
  - Assisted with reconciling and structuring claim documentation for an Enterprise Singapore grant supporting an EsoGLOVE clinical trial, consolidating vendor invoices into an auditor-ready format.
  - Built contact-recovery and CRM-style automation (Google Apps Script) for Viely, including a patient follow-up system and sizing-data analysis.
  - Coordinated smaller operational tasks such as component sourcing and courier logistics for medical device shipments to customers.
